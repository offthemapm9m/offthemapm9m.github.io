📍 About the channel

Off The Map is a faceless geography channel about the places the map doesn't explain — borders drawn wrong, towns that shouldn't exist, and lines on a page that changed the world anyway. Every episode takes one strange corner of the map and walks through how it got that way. Weird geography, explained: no hype, just the story behind the lines.
